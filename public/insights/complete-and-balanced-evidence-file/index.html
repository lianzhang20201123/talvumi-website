What Evidence Supports a Complete-and-Balanced Pet Food Claim?

Published 2026-08-05Updated 2026-08-05TALVUMI Market Desk

The claim should point to an accepted nutritional substantiation route and the exact formula being sold. This answer-first guide shows buyers what to verify before a commercial decision.

For importers, distributors and pet retail operators evaluating a market launch.

Direct answer

The claim should point to an accepted nutritional substantiation route and the exact formula being sold.

The controlling requirement depends on the destination market and the exact product. Buyers should use current local professional review before registration, label release or import.

Buyer checklist

Ask for the adequacy basis, life stage, formula code and review date.

The record should identify the SKU, market, owner, evidence source and version so buyers can compare the correct commercial product.
